Overview

Built on precision,
managed with discipline.

Delta Echo Group is the umbrella for a portfolio of operating businesses and investment vehicles founded by Daniel Eisner. Headquartered in South Florida, the group brings an operator's mindset to every asset class it enters — from twin-engine aviation to quantitative trading.

Each entity under the group operates independently with its own balance sheet, governance, and objectives. The holding structure provides strategic direction, shared infrastructure, and capital allocation oversight — without bureaucracy.

We don't chase trends. We acquire capability, build durable operations, and let compounding do the work.
